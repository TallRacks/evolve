# Music Pipeline

Workspace and platform Release lists use an internal pipeline representation derived from authoritative Release, Task, and Campaign records. Artist portal and developer serializers remain unchanged and do not expose internal task or campaign state.

Pipeline rows show Artist, type, date/countdown, track count, generic Task completion, Campaign state, and Release lifecycle status. Release 360 retains tracklist, task/checklist workflow, credits, campaigns, links, documents, rights context through Track detail, and Activity.

Deliverables are represented through the existing generic Task and checklist architecture. Evolve does not impose universal legal or distribution templates in this phase.
