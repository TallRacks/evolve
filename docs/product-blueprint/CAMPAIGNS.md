# Campaigns and Rollouts

Campaign strategy remains separate from Rollout execution.

Campaign responsibilities are explicit phase assignments with Lead or Support roles. Each assignment references an active Membership in the Campaign organization, is unique per Campaign/member/phase/role, and is changed only through permission-checked, audited services. Assignment does not grant authorization.

The Campaign detail screen presents a responsive phase matrix and real assign/remove actions for users with `campaign.manage`. Platform read views remain separate. Rollouts retain milestones, priorities, owners, due dates, dependencies, status transitions, completion, and progress. The current Critical Path meaning is operational: urgent/high, blocked, dependency-sensitive, or date-sensitive work; no CPM scheduling claim is made.
