# Mobile account and removal policy

Evolve users belong to organizations and may be referenced by operational,
approval, notification, and audit history. User deactivation is therefore
distinct from deleting organization records. Deactivation must revoke native
access while preserving required business history.

An eventual in-app “Request account removal” flow requires product, organization,
retention, and legal review. It must not automatically delete organization,
financial, contract, rights, or audit history. Organization/platform
administrators remain responsible for membership and record retention.

This is preparation only and is not a final legal or App Store policy decision.
